    历史发现 | Historical Discovery

    There is a curious reflection nebula in Monoceros that, when photographed, looks like the head of a match just beginning to ignite. It is called NGC 2261, and it is one of the most unusual objects in the Caldwell Catalog.

    在麒麟座中有一片奇特的反射星云，当被拍摄时，它看起来像一根刚刚开始点燃的火柴头。它被称为NGC 2261，是考德威尔星表中最为特殊的深空天体之一。

    William Herschel discovered this fan-shaped glow on 26 December 1783, describing it as "considerably bright and fan shaped, about 2 minutes long from the center." The nebulous object includes R Monocerotis, a 10th-magnitude "star" at the southern tip of the fan.

    Considerably bright and fan shaped About 2 minutes long from the center. (H IV-2)

    相当明亮且呈扇形，自中心向外延伸约2角分。（H IV-2）

    威廉·赫歇尔于1783年12月26日发现了这个扇形光晕，将其描述为"相当明亮且呈扇形，自中心向外延伸约2角分"。这片朦胧的天体包含麒麟座R——位于扇形结构南端一颗10等的"恒星"。

    While using the 6-inch refractor at Athens Observatory in 1861, director J. F. Julius Schmidt discovered that R Mon fluctuates in brightness at irregular intervals. Historically, the star has varied between magnitude 9.5 and 13.0.

    1861年，希腊雅典天文台台长J·F·尤利乌斯·施密特在使用6英寸折射望远镜时发现，麒麟座R的亮度会以不规则的间隔波动。历史上这颗恒星的亮度变化范围在9.5至13.0等之间。

    哈勃的发现 | Hubble's Discovery

    While comparing photographs of NGC 2261 taken between 1900 and 1916, American astronomer Edwin Hubble found that the nebula's shape and brightness also vary over time scales ranging from a few weeks to months.

    美国天文学家埃德温·哈勃在对比1900年至1916年间拍摄的NGC 2261照片时发现，这片星云的形态和亮度会在从数周至数月不等的时间尺度上发生变化。

    Ever since that discovery, NGC 2261 has been called Hubble's Variable Nebula, leading many to believe that Hubble actually discovered the nebula. Out of respect for Sir William, I like to call NGC 2261 "Herschel's Forgotten Fan."

    自该发现以来，NGC 2261便被称为哈勃变光星云，这导致许多人误以为哈勃是该星云的发现者。出于对威廉·赫歇尔爵士的敬意，我更愿将NGC 2261称为"赫歇尔被遗忘的扇状星云"。

    Inspired by Hubble's discovery, Carl Otto Lampland began in 1916 to monitor the nebula with the 42-inch reflector at Lowell Observatory. By 1951 he had produced a valuable series of 900 photographs.

    受哈勃发现的启发，卡尔·奥托·兰普兰于1916年开始使用洛厄尔天文台的42英寸反射望远镜对星云进行监测。到1951年时，他已拍摄了900张极具科研价值的系列照片。

    Like snapshots of a candle flame flickering in the wind, these images showed the nebula's shape and intensity changing over the years.

    这些影像如同风中摇曳的烛火快照，清晰记录了星云形态与亮度的逐年变化。

    神秘的阴影 | Shadows of Mystery

    Curiously, the variations in the nebula did not coincide with the irregular fluctuations of the "star" that supposedly illuminated it. Furthermore, individual details in the nebula could be "displaced" by as much as 1 arcsecond in a mere four days.

    Clearly, Lampland contended, such changes could not be related to physical movements of material, since that would imply matter being propelled across space at supernatural velocities.

    兰普兰坚称，这种变化显然与物质的物理运动无关，否则就意味着物质正以超自然的速度穿越太空。

    值得注意的是，星云的变化规律与理论上为其提供照明的"恒星"不规则光变并不吻合。更令人费解的是，星云内部某些结构特征的位置偏移量在短短四天内就能达到1角秒。

    Lampland concluded that dark shadows—like those cast by the Moon onto the Earth during solar eclipses—were sweeping across the nebula to cause the observed effects. Such shadows, he believed, were cast by clouds of dust traveling very close to the nebula's light source.

    兰普兰得出结论：如同日食时月球在地球上投下的阴影，正是横扫星云的暗影导致了观测到的明暗变化。他认为这些阴影是由紧邻星云光源运动的尘埃云投射形成的。

    茧状星云 | The Cocoon Nebula

    In 1959, George Herbig used the Lick Observatory's 120-inch reflector and found R Monocerotis to be a tiny triangular nebula "about 5 arcseconds long, increasing in surface brightness toward the southern apex, fading out smoothly to the north, but with sharp, well-defined edges on east and west. No star could be seen."

    1959年，乔治·赫比格在利克天文台120英寸反射望远镜的优异视宁度条件下进行目视观测，发现麒麟座R实为一个微小的三角状星云，"长度约5角秒，南部顶点处表面亮度递增，向北逐渐平滑衰减，但东西两侧边缘锐利分明"。未见恒星踪影。
