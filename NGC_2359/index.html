    雷神头盔星云 · Thor's Helmet (NGC 2359)

    参数 / Parameter 数值 / Value
    类型 / Type 发射/反射星云 / Emission/Reflection Nebula
    星座 / Constellation 大犬座 / Canis Major
    赤经 / Right Ascension 07h 18.6m
    赤纬 / Declination -13° 12′
    视星等 / Apparent Magnitude – (评级 3.5 / Rating 3.5)
    视直径 / Angular Size 9′ × 6′
    距离 / Distance 约 1,600 光年 / ~1,600 light-years
    中心恒星 / Central Star HD 56925 (WR7) 沃尔夫-拉叶星
    发现者 / Discoverer 威廉·赫歇尔 / William Herschel (1785)

    The region of sky 10° northeast of Alpha Canis Majoris (Sirius), the brightest star in the heavens, contains a rich stream of telescopic nebulae and clusters that can keep visual observers and CCD imagers occupied for nights on end. This region is one of my favorite winter haunts, and its wonders continually amaze me. NGC 2359 is one of the more remarkable nebulae in that region.

    天狼星（大犬座α星）东北方10°的天区中，蕴藏着一条由众多望远镜可见的星云与星团组成的富集带，足以让目视观测者与CCD成像者彻夜忙碌。这片区域是我冬季最钟爱的观测胜地，其奇观总令我惊叹不已。NGC 2359便是该区域最引人注目的星云之一。

    "I don't know how this beauty escaped my attention prior to the creation of the Secret Deep list. Nevertheless, this fantastic wash of nebulosity has numerous subtle structures visible to the eye, which blossom into a wonderland of delight when imaged through a CCD."

    "在编制'深空秘境'清单前，我竟未曾留意到这般瑰丽天体。然而这片绚烂的星云物质中，肉眼即可辨识诸多精微结构，当通过CCD成像时，更会绽放出令人沉醉的奇幻景致。"

    For some odd reason, I always thought this nebula was a deep southern object, but in fact, NGC 2359 lies a bit farther north than open clusters M46 and M47 in neighboring Puppis. In his book Astronomical Objects for Southern Telescopes, Ernst J. Hartung notes that 51 years after his father's discovery of the object, John Herschel compared it to "the profile of a bust (head, neck and shoulders)."

    出于某种奇怪的原因，我一直以为这个星云是南天深处的天体，但事实上，NGC 2359位于邻近船尾座的疏散星团M46和M47稍偏北的位置。在《南天望远镜观测天体》一书中，恩斯特·J·哈通提到，在其父亲发现该天体51年后，约翰·赫歇尔将其比作"半身像的轮廓（头部、颈部与肩部）"。

    "Indeed, as you can see from the table above, the nebula has all manner of fanciful names; I like the 'Flying Eye' moniker the best, since it spotlights the beautiful bubble of gas (the eye) nestled within wide wings of associated nebulosity."

    "诚然，如上表所示，这个星云拥有各种充满想象力的名称；我最偏爱'飞眼'这个称谓，因为它突显了嵌套在广阔羽状星云中的美丽气泡状结构（即'眼睛'）。"

    The nebula has acquired many whimsical names over the years—Thor's Helmet, Flying Eye, Duck Head Nebula—but all these epithets point to the same breathtaking cosmic structure: a colossal bubble of ionized gas sculpted by the fierce radiation and stellar winds of a dying giant star. It's quite a remarkable sight in color CCD images, and one of the more exotic-looking nebulae in the sky.

    这些年来，这个星云获得了许多充满想象力的名称——雷神头盔、飞眼、鸭头星云——但所有这些称谓都指向同一片令人叹为观止的宇宙构造：一个由垂死巨星强烈的辐射与星风雕琢而成的巨型电离气体气泡。在彩色CCD图像中，它呈现出相当惊人的景象，堪称天空中最具异域风情的星云之一。

    "I observed January 31, 1785: A broad extended nebulosity, forms a parallelogram with a ray southwards; the parallelogram is 8' long, 6' wide, very faint." — William Herschel (H V-21)

    "[1785年1月31日观测] 一片广阔延展的星云，与一道向南的射线构成平行四边形；该平行四边形长8'，宽6'，极为暗淡。" — 威廉·赫歇尔 (H V-21)

    NGC 2359 is an emission nebula excited by the powerful wind (about 2,000 km/sec) and chemically enriched radiation of the 11.4-magnitude Wolf-Rayet Star HD 56925 (WR7). Wolf-Rayet stars represent a rare evolutionary phase in the lives of massive stars during which they undergo heavy mass loss, typically on the order of 10⁻⁵ solar mass per year. Due to the chemically enriched radiation, they display an extraordinary spectrum dominated by emission lines of highly ionized elements.

    NGC 2359是一个由11.4等沃尔夫-拉叶星HD 56925（WR7）的强大星风（约2,000公里/秒）及化学增丰辐射激发的发射星云。沃尔夫-拉叶星代表了大质量恒星生命中的一个罕见演化阶段，在此期间它们经历剧烈的质量损失，通常每年约10⁻⁵个太阳质量。由于其化学增丰辐射，这类恒星展现出以高度电离元素的发射线为主导的异常光谱。

    During its main-sequence phase, the 25 solar mass HD 56925 carved a huge bubble into a rather dense warm molecular cloud (Sharpless 298) that partially bounds it. The 2.3-million-year-old ring-like nebula is centered on HD 56925 and has a mass between 700 and 2,400 Suns. At an accepted distance of 1,600 light-years, the filamentary bubble has a true linear extent of about 230 × 120 light-years. Strong winds streaming from the extremely hot (up to ~50,000 K) Type O central star are driving the bubble's expansion at about 12 km/sec.
