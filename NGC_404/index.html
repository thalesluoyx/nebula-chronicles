    天体概述 | Object Overview

    NGC 404, affectionately known as "Mirach's Ghost," is a lenticular galaxy that presents one of the most tantalizing challenges in the night sky for amateur astronomers. Its fame derives not from luminosity or intricate structure, but from an extraordinary circumstance of cosmic geography—the galaxy sits a mere 7 arcminutes from Mirach (Beta Andromedae), a brilliant orange-hued star of magnitude 2.05 that dominates its immediate neighborhood like a streetlamp overwhelming a distant city.

    NGC 404的魅力来自一种奇妙的宇宙地理——这个透镜状星系距离仙女座β星奎宿九仅7角分，那颗视星等达2.05等的橙红色亮星如同街灯一般遮蔽了它微弱的光芒。

    "A ghostly wisp of light, forever doomed to haunt the vicinity of a brilliant stellar beacon."

    一道幽灵般的光痕，注定永远徘徊在一颗璀璨恒星的附近。

    The galaxy itself is a modest member of the NGC 1023 group of galaxies, a collection of approximately 13 galaxies gravitationally bound together some 10 million light-years away. At this distance, NGC 404's 3.4-arcminute span translates to roughly 10,000 light-years across—roughly comparable to our own Milky Way's diameter. Its classification as an S0 (lenticular) galaxy places it at an evolutionary crossroads between spiral and elliptical galaxies, possessing both a disk structure and a central bulge, yet showing no evidence of the grand spiral arms that define galaxies like our own Milky Way or the Andromeda Galaxy itself.

    该星系是NGC 1023星系群中一位谦逊的成员——这个由约13个星系组成的引力束缚系统距离我们约1000万光年。在这个距离上，NGC 404的3.4角分跨度相当于约1万光年的实际直径。作为透镜状（S0型）星系，它处于旋涡星系与椭圆星系之间的演化十字路口：既有盘状结构与中央核球，又缺乏如银河系或仙女座大星系那般壮丽的旋臂。

    "These galaxies appear like luminous lentils, showing a central concentration but lacking spiral structure."

    这些星系呈现为发光的透镜状天体，具有中央聚集特征却缺乏旋臂结构。

    历史发现 | Historical Discovery

    The discovery of NGC 404 in 1784 by William Herschel must be considered remarkable given the overwhelming presence of Mirach in the same field of view. Herschel, working from his observatory at Datchet, England, noted the object as a "faint nebula" whose nature remained uncertain for well over a century.

    1784年，威廉·赫歇尔在英格兰达特福特的观象台发现了NGC 404——考虑到同一视野中被光芒完全压制的奎宿九，这一发现着实令人惊叹。这位恒星搜寻先驱将其记录为一团"暗淡的星云"，而它的真实本质在随后一个多世纪里始终是个谜。

    w. h e r s c h e l: [Observed October 27, 1784] A very faint star, with a very faint, small nebulosity, strongly suspected. (H V-22)

    赫歇尔：[1784年10月27日观测] 一颗暗淡的恒星，周围伴随极其微弱的星云状物质，引人强烈怀疑。(H V-22)

    Modern astrophysics has since revealed that NGC 404 is not merely a passive galactic specimen. Observations with the Hubble Space Telescope have uncovered complex structures within its central regions, including an inner ring of star formation and an unusual "pseudobulge"—a region of concentrated stars that may have formed through processes other than traditional galactic bulge formation. The galaxy also hosts a low-ionization nuclear emission-line region (LINER), suggesting modest activity around its central supermassive black hole, estimated at roughly 100,000 solar masses.

    现代天体物理学揭示，NGC 404绝非一个消极的星系标本。哈勃空间望远镜的观测发现其中心区域存在复杂结构，包括一个内部恒星形成环和一个不同寻常的"伪核球"——这片恒星聚集区可能并非通过传统核球形成过程产生。该星系还拥有一个低电离核发射线区（LINER），暗示其中心超大质量黑洞（估计约10万太阳质量）周围存在微弱活动。

    仙女座的隐藏宝石 | Sculptor of the Hidden Gem

    The constellation Andromeda stretches across the autumn sky of the Northern Hemisphere, anchored by three brilliant stars—Alpheratz, Mirach, and Almach—that form the characteristic "Great Square" asterism's eastern side. While many observers focus on the constellation's most celebrated denizens—the spectacular Andromeda Galaxy (M31) and its satellite companions—NGC 404 rewards those who linger in the shadow of Mirach.

    仙女座横跨北半球秋季夜空，以三颗亮星——壁宿二、奎宿九和天大将军一——为标志，构成秋季四边形东侧的经典形态。当众多观测者将目光投向仙女座最负盛名的天体——壮丽的仙女座大星系（M31）及其卫星伙伴时，NGC 404却在奎宿九的阴影中静候那些愿意驻足的人。

    "Andromeda offers more than M31; those who linger find hidden treasures."

    仙女座所奉献的远不止M31；那些愿意驻足的人会发现隐藏的珍宝。

    Unlike the dramatic objects that populate the Caldwell Catalogue—those bold nebulae, brilliant star clusters, and galaxies that demand attention through sheer magnificence—Mirach's Ghost requires patience, technique, and a careful eye.
