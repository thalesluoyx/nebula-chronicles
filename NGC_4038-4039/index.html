    触须星系 · The Antennae Galaxies (NGC 4038/4039)

    When sweeping through the constellation of Corvus with his powerful reflecting telescope on the evening of February 7, 1785, William Herschel stumbled upon something extraordinary—a celestial sight that would eventually become one of the most iconic objects in our night sky. He recorded his observation with characteristic brevity: "Pretty bright, large opening with a branch, or two nebulae very faintly joined."

    1785年2月7日的那个夜晚，威廉·赫歇尔操控着他那架强大的反射望远镜扫过乌鸦座时，偶然发现了一个非凡的天体——一个最终将成为夜空中最具标志性的观测目标。他以一贯的简洁风格记录下这次观测："相当明亮，巨大开口处延伸出分支，实为两个微弱相连的星云。"

    "Large opening with a branch, or two nebulae very faintly joined." "巨大开口处延伸出分支，实为两个微弱相连的星云。"

    Little did Herschel know that evening that what he had perceived as two faint nebulae were actually two massive spiral galaxies locked in a cosmic death grip—galaxies that would later be known as NGC 4038 (Caldwell 60) and NGC 4039 (Caldwell 61), the magnificent Antennae Galaxies.

    赫歇尔当时绝不会想到，他眼中这两个模糊的星云实际上是两个巨大的旋涡星系，正陷入一场宇宙级别的死亡纠缠——这对星系后来被编为NGC 4038（考德维尔60号）和NGC 4039（考德维尔61号），即壮美的触须星系。

    The Cosmic Collision · 宇宙碰撞

    Imagine Herschel's astonishment had he somehow learned the truth about what he had discovered: two extragalactic cannibals, two savage galaxies ripping each other apart—each disemboweling billions of stars from the other and flinging them across several hundred million light-years of space, where they continue to shine like luminous entrails.

    试想倘若赫歇尔有朝一日得知他发现的真相会作何反应：他无意间窥见的是两头河外星系的"食人族"——两个狂暴的星系正在互相撕扯，彼此从对方体内扯出数十亿颗恒星，将它们抛洒到数亿光年的太空中，这些恒星如同发光的内脏碎片般持续闪耀。

    "two extragalactic cannibals, two savage galaxies ripping each other apart" "两头河外星系的食人族——两个狂暴的星系正在互相撕扯"

    Computer simulations have revealed the mechanism behind this spectacular display. When two spiral galaxies collide, their constituent stars do not smash together like cars on a highway—the distances between individual stars are simply too vast for direct collisions. Instead, what ensues is a battle of gravitational tides that ultimately disrupts each galactic system.

    计算机模拟揭示了这一壮观景象背后的物理机制。当两个旋涡星系相遇时，它们的组成恒星并不会像公路上的汽车那样迎头相撞——毕竟恒星间的距离实在太过遥远。真正发生的是一场引力潮汐的激烈较量，最终瓦解了两个星系的结构。

    As the galaxies' respective nuclei move apart from one another, tides pull billions of stars from each spiral's disk. The galaxies lose mass and, with it, some of the gravitational pull that once held them together. The orphaned stars flee unimpeded into space, forming two taffy-like streamers—the famous "antennae" that give this galaxy pair its name.

    随着两个星系的核心彼此远离，潮汐力从每个星系的旋涡盘中剥离出数十亿颗恒星。星系质量不断流失，维系自身的引力也随之减弱。被抛离的恒星无拘无束地逃入太空，形成两条太妃糖般的星流长尾——这就是这对星系得名的著名"触须"。

    Starburst Symphony · 星暴交响曲

    The Astrophysical Journal reported in April 1997 that massive star formation has been triggered by this extragalactic war. X-ray images from the ROSAT satellite reveal that the most intense X-ray emission comes from each galaxy's nucleus and very knotty arms—likely from scores of X-ray binaries and supernova remnants.

    1997年4月，《天体物理学杂志》刊载的研究报告证实，这场河外战争已触发大规模恒星形成。罗萨特卫星的X射线图像显示，最强烈的X射线辐射源自两个星系的核区及高度结节的旋臂——这些辐射很可能来自数十个X射线双星系统和超新星遗迹。

    The duo has also witnessed recently recorded supernova activity, with two explosions within the last century—one in 1921 and another in 1974. Supernova hunters should keep a watchful eye on this active pair.

    这对星系在上世纪已记录到两次超新星爆发——一次在1921年，另一次在1974年。超新星猎手们应当密切关注这个活跃的星系对。

    "The radiation may be coming from scores of X-ray binaries and supernova remnants." "这些辐射可能源自数十个X射线双星系统和超新星遗迹。"

    More spectacularly, the Hubble Space Telescope has revealed a brilliant fireworks show within the dynamic duo. Hubble uncovered more than 1,000 bright, young star clusters bursting to life as a result of the collision. The brightest of these clusters contains roughly a million stars, all just a few million years old.

    更为壮观的是，哈勃太空望远镜在这对活跃双星内部揭示了一场璀璨的烟火表演。哈勃发现了超过1000个明亮的新生星团正在碰撞中爆发式形成。其中最耀眼的星团包含约百万颗恒星，年龄均仅有数百万年。

    Images from Kitt Peak's 2.1-meter telescope have helped astronomers identify blue knots of star formation in the distorted galaxy disks, some older stellar populations in their tattered bulges, and a large dust complex at the apparent interface between the two disks.

    基特峰国家天文台2.1米望远镜拍摄的图像帮助天文学家识别出扭曲星系盘中的蓝色恒星形成结，它们破碎核球中的一些较老恒星群体，以及两个星系盘明显交界处的一个大型尘埃复合体。
