    南蜂窝星团 · Southern Beehive (NGC 2516)

    Like rivets, bright open clusters seem to fasten the Milky Way to Carina, the Keel of the Ship Argo. And among the most prominent is NGC 2516. This 4th-magnitude cluster lies just 3½° southwest of Epsilon (ε) Carinae (Avior), the 2nd-magnitude star marking the foot of the False Cross; the long axis of the Cross points almost right to it. Abbe Nicolas Louis de Lacaille discovered the cluster during his pioneering southern-sky survey. It is the third Class II object (nebulous clusters) in his 1755 catalog, and he recorded it as a "very close group of 10-12 stars." (The "nebulosity" in this case was not dust or gas but unresolved starlight.) John Herschel saw "perhaps 200 to 250 stars" centered on an orange 8th-magnitude star.

    如同铆钉般，明亮的疏散星团仿佛将银河系牢牢固定在船底座的船龙骨上。其中最为醒目的当属NGC 2516。这个4等星团就位于船底座ε星（Avior，2等星）西南方向仅3½度处——这颗标志着伪十字底部的亮星；而伪十字的长轴几乎正指向它。修道院院长尼古拉·路易·德·拉卡伊在进行开创性的南天巡天时发现了这个星团。在其1755年编制的星表中，这是第三类II型天体（朦胧星团），他描述其为"由10-12颗恒星组成的极密集星群"。（此处的"朦胧"并非尘埃或气体所致，而是未解析的星光。）约翰·赫歇尔观测到"约200至250颗恒星围绕着一颗8等的橙色恒星"。

    "An orange coloured star 8th magnitude, in middle of a large and magnificent cluster of perhaps 200 to 250 stars [of] 8th to 16th magnitude."

    "一颗8等的橙色恒星，位于一个由约200至250颗8至16等恒星组成的大型壮观星团中央。"

    NGC 2516 is an easy naked-eye target. It is sometimes mistakenly referred to as the Southern Pleiades, but that name has already been claimed. IC 2602 (Caldwell 102) — also in Carina, nearly 20° to the east-southeast — has been known as the Southern Pleiades ever since Lacaille commented on its similarity to that northern wonder.

    NGC 2516是一个肉眼可见的易观测目标。该星团有时被误称为"南天昴星团"，但这个名称实际上已被正式用于指代另一目标。据称，IC 2602（考德维尔102）——同样位于船底座，向东南偏东方向延伸近20度——自拉卡伊指出其与北方奇观昴星团的相似性以来，一直被称为"南天昴星团"。

    When I surveyed the literature on NGC 2516, I discovered a trend: the cluster's reported angular size has been diminishing over the years. In his Celestial Handbook, Robert Burnham Jr. describes NGC 2516 as a "brilliant group, easily visible to the naked eye, with more than 100 stars scattered over a field 1° in diameter." The first edition of Sky Atlas 2000.0 (some versions of which mislabel the cluster as NGC 2561) represents this view fairly well, showing the cluster as a 1°-wide circle centered on a dense core of starlight.

    在查阅关于NGC 2516的文献资料时，我发现了一个趋势：该星团报告的角直径尺寸正逐年缩小。罗伯特·伯纳姆在其《天体手册》中将NGC 2516描述为"一个明亮的星群，肉眼清晰可见，超过100颗恒星散布在直径1度的天区内"。第一版《天空图鉴2000.0》（某些版本误将该星团标记为NGC 2561）较好地呈现了这一观点——将星团绘制为直径1度的圆形区域，中心是密集的恒星核心。

    "A brilliant group, easily visible to the naked eye, with more than 100 stars scattered over a field 1° in diameter."

    "一个明亮的星群，肉眼清晰可见，超过100颗恒星散布在直径1度的天区内。"

    When I went out and looked at the cluster with the naked eye, I saw a 1°-wide, wishbone-shaped grouping. Three 6th-magnitude stars in a gentle northeast-to-southwest curve formed the thin northwestern branch of the wishbone, while a fuzzy wedge of knitted starlight formed the thicker southeastern branch. The two segments are separated by about ½°.

    当我外出用肉眼观测这个星团时，我看到一个宽达1度的叉骨状星群。三颗6等星以平缓的东北-西南走向曲线构成了叉骨纤细的西北分支，而一团由交织星光组成的模糊楔形则形成了较粗的东南分支。这两个部分相距约半度。

    More recently, Steven Hynes and Brent Archinal have moved the cluster's center 7' to the north and shrunk the cluster's width by another 8' (from 30' to 22'). So in the last quarter century, NGC 2516's diameter has diminished by 63 percent.

    最近，史蒂文·海因斯和布伦特·阿奇纳尔将该星团中心位置向北移动了7角分，并缩小了其范围——宽度又缩小了8角分（从30角分减至22角分）。因此在过去25年间，NGC 2516的直径已缩减了63%。

    "Most of the stars are concentrated in a 20-30' diameter area, but possibly with outlier stars going out to 1°. So, depending on how one wants to define diameter, all the values cited are correct!"

    "虽然大部分恒星集中在直径20-30角分的区域内，但外围成员星可能分布至1度范围。因此根据不同的直径定义标准，所有引用的数值其实都是正确的！"

    Puzzled by this apparent dilemma, I referred to astrophysical journals and checked the Hipparcos data as well as the second edition of Sky Catalogue 2000.0. The Hipparcos satellite revealed that the middle star of the trio in the wishbone's northwestern branch is not a cluster member; it is only 53 light-years distant, while the cluster lies 1,300 light-years away. But the other two bright stars in that branch are at the cluster's distance, so they probably are members.

    面对这一看似矛盾的现象，我查阅了天体物理学期刊，核对了依巴谷卫星数据以及《Sky Catalogue 2000.0》第二版。依巴谷卫星的观测表明，位于许愿骨西北分支的三联星中，中间那颗并非该星团成员——其距离仅53光年，而该星团实际位于1,300光年之外。但该分支另外两颗亮星的距离与星团相符，应属成员星。

    Perhaps this exercise simply demonstrates just how difficult it is to determine the extent of an open cluster in a rich Milky Way region. It also highlights the imprecision of supposedly "hard" astronomical data.
