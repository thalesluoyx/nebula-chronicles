    爱斯基摩星云 · Eskimo Nebula (NGC 2392)

    基本参数 | Essential Data

    参数 / Parameter 数值 / Value
    类型 / Type 行星状星云 / Planetary Nebula
    星座 / Constellation 双子座 / Gemini
    赤经 / RA 07h 29m 10.8s
    赤纬 / Dec +20°54′43″
    视星等 / Magnitude 9.2
    视直径 / Apparent Size 47″×43″
    距离 / Distance ~4,000光年 / ~4,000 light-years
    发现者 / Discoverer 威廉·赫歇尔 / William Herschel
    发现时间 / Discovery Date 1787年1月17日 / 17 January 1787

    Through small telescopes, most planetary nebulae appear as pale shells or symmetrical patches of light. Their enchanting central stars—whose ionizing ultraviolet rays energize the nebulae—usually lie just beyond the grasp of normal vision. But NGC 2392 in Gemini is an exception. Not only is it bright, being a mere 0.4 magnitude fainter than the famous Ring Nebula (M57) in Lyra, but its central star all but dominates the view. Actually it is somewhat misleading to say that NGC 2392, or any other planetary nebula for that matter, is "bright"; unlike every other type of deep-sky object, no planetary nebula is readily accessible to the unaided eye.

    透过小型望远镜观测，大多数行星状星云呈现为苍白的壳层或对称的光斑。那些迷人的中心恒星——其电离紫外线为星云提供能量——通常难以被肉眼直接捕捉。但双子座的NGC 2392是个例外。它不仅亮度高达7.3等（仅比天琴座著名的环状星云M57暗0.4个星等），其中心恒星更是几乎主宰了整个视野。

    NGC 2392 is unquestionably a telescopic wonder. Its singular appearance compelled the great William Herschel to call it "a very remarkable phenomenon."

    W. H E R S C H E L: [Observed 17 January 1787] A star of 9th magnitude with a pretty bright milky nebulosity all around. A very remarkable phenomenon. (H IV-45)

    NGC 2392无疑是一部望远镜奇观。其独特的外观令伟大的威廉·赫歇尔将其称为"非常显著的天象"。

    威廉·赫歇尔：[1787年1月17日观测] 一颗9等星，周围环绕着相当明亮的乳白色星云。这是非常显著的天象。（H IV-45）

    His son John examined the nebula with an 18.5-inch reflector and agreed; he saw it as an 8th-magnitude star "exactly in the center of an exactly round bright atmosphere." His use of the word "atmosphere" was appropriate for the times. In 1755 the Prussian philosopher and physicist Immanuel Kant hypothesized that the Sun and planets were born from a rotating cloud of gas and dust.

    他的儿子约翰用18.5英寸反射镜观测了这个星云，并深表赞同；他将其描述为一颗8等星"恰好位于正圆形明亮大气层的正中心"。当时使用"大气层"一词是符合时代背景的。1755年，普鲁士哲学家兼物理学家伊曼努尔·康德提出假说，认为太阳和行星诞生自旋转的气体和尘埃云团。

    By the 19th century, when John Herschel was turning his attention to his father's discoveries, geologists were using Kant's nebular hypothesis to explain the formation of the Earth. In his 1914 work The Problem of Volcanism, Joseph Iddings explains that geologists of the 19th century conceived a nebula as a "homogeneous gas revolving about a central nucleus and separating into annular zones that were supposed to condense into the planets and satellites of the solar system."

    到了19世纪，当约翰·赫歇尔开始关注其父的发现时，地质学家们正运用康德的星云假说来解释地球的形成。约瑟夫·伊丁斯在1914年著作《火山活动问题》中指出，19世纪地质学家将星云构想为"围绕中央核体旋转的均质气体，这些气体会分离成环状带，并最终凝聚成太阳系的行星与卫星"。

    Any views they might have had of NGC 2392, with its multiple shells and bright central core, certainly would have excited them.

    若他们得以观测到拥有多重壳层与明亮核心的NGC 2392，定会为之振奋。

    Of course, Kant was right in his thinking, as we know today, but the evidence that would ultimately verify his theory would not be found in planetary nebulae, which have nothing to do with planets or their formation.

    当然，正如我们今天所知，康德的构想是正确的——但最终验证其理论的证据不会出现在行星状星云中，因为行星状星云与行星及其形成过程毫无关联。

    NGC 2392's true grandeur surfaced with the advent of photography. Photographic plates—especially those taken with large telescopes in red or ultraviolet light—show the nebula's concentric shells primped with fine details. The central star lies at the core of a bizarre gyroscopic arrangement of bright gas, which in turn is surrounded by an annular fanfare of radial streaks.

    随着摄影技术的出现，NGC 2392的真实壮丽才得以展现。感光底片——尤其是那些用大型望远镜在红光或紫外光波段拍摄的影像——揭示了该星云精致的同心壳层结构。中央恒星位于一个奇特陀螺状明亮气体结构的核心区域，外围环绕着呈放射状排列的环状辉光。

    Such a sight compels our eyes to search for patterns, just as they would in the body and folds of a cloud in tumult. Once ignited, our imagination transforms the nebulous patches into something fanciful, like a face. Indeed, NGC 2392's most popular nickname is the Eskimo Nebula because of the way it resembles a face peering out from a fur-lined parka.

    这种景象会激发人类寻找图案的本能，就像我们在翻滚的云层中辨识形体那样。一旦想象力被点燃，这些朦胧的光斑就会幻化成奇妙的形象，比如一张人脸。事实上，由于该星云酷似从毛皮风雪帽中探出的人脸，NGC 2392最广为人知的昵称正是"爱斯基摩星云"。

    Ultraviolet-light photographs tend to exaggerate the "nose" (the planetary's hot central star), thus the nebula's other popular nickname, the Clown-Face. Of course, we see what our own imaginations dictate. In his Celestial Handbook Robert Burnham Jr. says the "whole nebula irresistibly suggests the classic and unforgettable features" of the late comedian W. C. Fields.

    照片往往会夸大其"鼻子"（即行星状星云
