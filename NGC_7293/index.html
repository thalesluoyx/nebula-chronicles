    螺旋星云 · Helix Nebula (NGC 7293)

    参数 Parameters 值 Value
    类型 Type 行星状星云 Planetary Nebula
    星座 Constellation 宝瓶座 Aquarius
    赤经 Right Ascension 22h 29m 38.4s
    赤纬 Declination −20° 50′ 13″
    视星等 Apparent Magnitude 7.3; 6.0 (O'Meara)
    视直径 Apparent Size 12′ × 10′
    距离 Distance 522 光年 light-years
    发现者 Discoverer 格奥尔格·冯·奥威尔斯 Georg Friedrich Julius Arthur von Auwers (1862)

    历史发现 · Historical Discovery

    The eclectic German astronomer Georg Friedrich Julius Arthur von Auwers (1838-1915) is most noted for his work on Procyon's proper motion; his observations of Venus's 1874 and 1882 transits across the face of the Sun; and his discovery of Τ Scorpii, a nova in the globular cluster M80. But he also published, in 1862, a catalog of William Herschel's nebulae and clusters — a work he began preparing for when he was only 16 years old.

    德国天文学家格奥尔格·弗里德里希·尤利乌斯·阿瑟·冯·奥威尔斯（1838-1915）以三项成就闻名于世：其一，对南河三自行运动的研究；其二，观测1874年与1882年金星凌日现象；其三，发现球状星团M80中的新星天蝎座Τ。但鲜为人知的是，这位学者早在16岁便着手编纂威廉·赫歇尔星云星团表，并于1862年将其发表于《柯尼斯堡天文观测》第34卷。

    "Printed in Volume 34 of the Konigsberg Observations, the work also includes a list of 52 'very widely diffused nebulosities,' including von Auwers's discovery of an immense object in Aquarius."

    "该著作还收录了52个'极度弥散的星云状天体'，其中包括他在宝瓶座发现的巨大天体。"

    That object is recognized today as the largest and closest planetary nebula known in the heavens — NGC 7293, the famous Helical or Helix Nebula. For his contributions to science, a lunar crater has been named in von Auwers's honor.

    这个如今被认定为人类已知最大且最近的行星状星云：NGC 7293，即著名的螺旋星云（Helix Nebula）。为表彰其科学贡献，月球上一座环形山以"冯·奥威尔斯"命名。

    观测之谜 · The Observation Enigma

    The Helix lies about 11° northwest of 1st-magnitude Alpha (α) Piscis Austrini (Fomalhaut) and 1° west of 5th-magnitude Nu (ν) Aquarii. Published visual magnitudes range from "6th" to 7.4.

    螺旋星云位于南鱼座一等星α（北落师门）西北约11度处，在宝瓶座ν星（5等星）以东1度处。已公布的目视星等在"6等"至7.4等之间。

    "Given the object's brightness and its proximity to the globular cluster M30 in Capricornus (the planetary is about 12° east-northeast of the cluster), one has to wonder how the greatest observers, from Charles Messier to John Herschel, failed to detect it."

    "考虑到这个行星状星云的亮度及其与摩羯座球状星团M30的邻近关系（该行星状星云位于星团东北偏东方约12度处），人们不禁要问：从查尔斯·梅西耶到约翰·赫歇尔这些伟大的观测者为何都未能发现它？"

    But the answer is simple. First, the Helix is enormous, spanning an impressive 12′ × 10′ on the sky. Second, it has a low surface brightness. Third, its center is a gaping "hole." And finally, the object is most readily apparent with binoculars or a rich-field telescope.

    答案其实很简单。首先，螺旋星云尺寸巨大，在天空中横跨12角分×10角分的惊人范围；其次，其表面亮度较低；第三，中心区域存在明显的"空洞"；最后，该天体最适宜用双筒望远镜或广角望远镜观测。

    "By condensing its light, such instruments increase the contrast between the nebula and the sky background, making it easier to see."

    "这类仪器通过聚光增强了星云与天空背景的对比度，使其更易于观测。"

    一生的追逐 · A Lifetime Pursuit

    Throughout my childhood, the Helix was one of my visual nemeses. It was right up there with M74, that large and diffuse galaxy in Pisces. I grew up in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and did most of my observing with either a 4¼-inch long-focus reflector or the 9-inch Clark refractor at Harvard College Observatory. For 20 years I searched for the Helix on the best nights, when the object was high above the horizon, but I had two strikes against me: city lights and a limited field of view.

    在我的童年时期，螺旋星云（Helix）始终是我目视观测的劲敌之一——它与双鱼座中那个巨大而弥散的M74星系并列成为最难观测的目标。我在马萨诸塞州剑桥市长大，最常使用的观测设备要么是一台4英寸长焦反射镜，要么是哈佛大学天文台的9英寸克拉克折射镜。整整二十年里，每逢最佳观测夜当该天体高悬于地平线上时，我都会尝试寻找螺旋星云，但我有两个不利因素：城市灯光和有限的视野范围。

    "I did not succeed in seeing the Helix until July 27, 1997, after I started the Caldwell project under the dark skies of Volcano, Hawaii."

    "直到1997年7月27日，当我在夏威夷火山镇漆黑的夜空下开始考德维尔深空天体观测计划时，才首次成功观测到螺旋星云。"

    So imagine my surprise (and disgust) when I raised a pair of 7×35 binoculars to the field and saw the Helix without a hitch! For the first time since childhood I felt a sense of primal awe and astonishment.

    因此当我举起7×35双筒望远镜对准那片天区，竟毫不费力地捕捉到它的身影时，那份震惊（与懊恼）简直难以言表！自童年以来，我第一次感受到那种原始的敬畏与震撼。

    "'It's glorious,' I penned. 'It's enormous. And with averted vision I suspect a hole.'"

    "'它壮丽非凡，'我在观测笔记中写道，'庞大的盘面占据视野。用余光观察时，我甚至能察觉到中央的空洞。'"
