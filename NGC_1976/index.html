    猎户座大星云 · The Great Orion Nebula (NGC 1976)

    关键参数 | Key Parameters

    参数 Parameter 中文 English
    类型 Type 发射星云与星团 Emission Nebula and Cluster
    所属星座 Constellation 猎户座 Orion
    赤经 Right Ascension 5h 35m.3 (四合星位置) 5h 35m.3 (Trapezium)
    赤纬 Declination -5° 23′ (四合星位置) -5° 23′ (Trapezium)
    星等 Magnitude 3.7 3.7
    视直径 Apparent Size 1°.5 × 1°.0 1°.5 × 1°.0
    距离 Distance 1,500 光年 1,500 light-years
    发现者 Discoverer 尼古拉·佩雷斯克 (1611) Nicolas Peiresc (1611)

    One of the greatest paradoxes in visual astronomy is that Galileo (who paid great attention to Orion, according to Webb) apparently never noticed the Great Nebula. Furthermore, there appears to be no mention of it in medieval records. Yet here is one of the grandest naked-eye nebulae in the heavens, dangling from one of the best-known asterisms (Orion's Belt) in one of the most famous and brilliant constellations.

    视觉天文学中最大的悖论之一便是：伽利略（据韦伯记载，他对猎户座极为关注）显然从未留意过这片壮丽的星云。更令人费解的是，中世纪文献中也未见任何相关记载。然而这片横亘天际的宏伟星云，不仅以裸眼即可观测，更垂悬于猎户座——这个举世闻名且璀璨夺目的星座——中最负盛名的星群（猎户腰带）之下。

    "There is one phenomenon among the fixed stars worthy of mention, which as far as I know, has hitherto been unnoticed by no one and indeed, cannot be well observed except with large telescopes." — Christiaan Huygens

    "在恒星之间存在一种值得关注的现象，据我所知至今尚未被世人注意，事实上，唯有借助大型望远镜方能清晰观测。" —— 克里斯蒂安·惠更斯

    Messier observed this nebula on March 4, 1769, and gave an extensive description of "the beautiful nebula in Orion's sword, around the star θ, which lies within it together with three other, fainter stars, which can be seen only with good instruments." He even included a carefully crafted drawing that appeared in the Memoires de l'Académie in 1771.

    梅西耶于1769年3月4日对这片星云进行了观测，并对"猎户座佩剑处美丽的星云"进行了详尽描述："它环绕着θ星，其内部还包含三颗仅能通过精密仪器观测的暗星。"他甚至附上了精心绘制的星图，收录于1771年《法兰西科学院纪要》第八图版之中。

    The Orion Nebula is an enormous cloud of fluorescent gas, predominantly hydrogen with traces of helium, carbon, nitrogen, and oxygen, spanning 40 light-years in diameter. It glows by the ultraviolet radiation streaming from Theta Orionis — a tight grouping of four massive stars whose light energy is burning a hole through the mackerel clouds of the nebula's sharply defined interior.

    猎户座大星云是一片巨大的荧光气体云团，主要由氢元素构成，并含有微量氦、碳、氮、氧元素，直径达40光年。其发光能量源自猎户座θ星——四颗大质量恒星组成的星群——放射的紫外线辐射。这些恒星释放的高能射线正在星云内部轮廓分明的斑驳云层中灼穿出一个空洞。

    Commonly called the Trapezium, these four jewels shine between 5th and 8th magnitude, and it is the brightest member that produces 99 percent of the energy that illuminates the clouds of gas we see through our telescopes.

    这四颗璀璨的恒星俗称"特拉佩兹四边形星团"，亮度介于5至8等之间，其中最亮的那颗提供了我们通过望远镜观测到的气体云发光所需99%的能量。

    All the hot young Trapezium stars probably began shining a mere 1 million years ago (our Sun is 4.5 billion years old). And there are about a thousand unseen members (300,000 to 1 million years old) hiding in the dense cloudscapes.

    所有炽热的年轻四边形恒星很可能仅在100万年前才开始发光（相较之下太阳已有45亿年历史）。另有约千颗形成于30万至100万年前的不可见恒星隐匿在浓密的云景之中。

    Yet, in how many New England winters have I looked across snow-laden fields to see this nebula in the middle of Orion's sword, looking like angel's breath against a frosted sky. Even today, M42 is visible with the naked eye from the heart of downtown Boston, Massachusetts!

    然而，在新英格兰的多少个冬日里，我曾眺望积雪覆盖的原野，目睹这把猎户之剑中央的星云，如同天使在冰霜天幕上呵出的气息。直至今日，马萨诸塞州波士顿市中心仍能用肉眼直接观测到M42星云！

    Recent Hubble Space Telescope images show the expansive nebula in unprecedented detail: tumultuous swirls of colorful churning gas; 40-billion-mile-long "comets" of dust and gas, whose comas enshroud newborn stars; dark protoplanetary disks silhouetted against the nebula's spectral glow. These images reveal a cosmic nursery where stars are being born in magnificent violence, their light painting the surrounding gas in hues of rose, violet, and electric blue.

    近期哈勃太空望远镜拍摄的图像以前所未有的细节展现了这片浩瀚星云：翻腾的彩色气体形成汹涌涡流、长达400亿英里的尘埃气体"彗星"其彗发包裹着新生恒星、以及逆着星光谱线显现轮廓的暗原行星盘。这些影像揭示了一座宇宙育婴室，恒星正在其中以壮丽而狂暴的方式诞生，它们发出的光芒将周围的气体染成玫瑰、紫罗兰与电光蓝的绚烂色彩。

    观测体验 | Observing Experience

    There is nothing quite like the first time you point a telescope toward the Orion Nebula on a crisp winter night. The view through the eyepiece is nothing short of transformative — what appears as a pale, ghostly smudge to the naked eye suddenly explodes into a three-dimensional tapestry of luminous clouds, dark dust lanes, and the brilliant Trapezium stars at its heart. Even a modest 4-inch telescope will reveal delicate wisps of nebulosity swirling around the central star cluster, with subtle color gradients hinting at the hot young stars hidden within.

    在清冽的冬夜将望远镜首次对准猎户座大星云，这种体验无可比拟。目镜中的景象堪称脱胎换骨——肉眼所见不过是苍白的幽灵般的模糊光斑，此刻却骤然绽放成一幅三维的锦绣画卷：发光的气体云、幽暗的尘埃带、以及中央璀璨夺目的四边形星团。即便是使用4英寸口径的小型望远镜，也能窥见星团周围萦绕的精致气态纤维，以及暗示着内部炽热新生恒星存在的微妙色彩层次。

    Under dark skies with a 10-inch or larger telescope, the nebula fills the field of view with breathtaking complexity. You can trace the sweeping curves of the bright "wings" extending north and south, follow the intricate dark dust structures that seem to carve channels through the glowing gas, and lose yourself in the endless detail
